## 🖤 Ishtar — my flagship

**[Ishtar](https://ishtar.numetal.xyz)** is an adults-only, text-only, **agent-mediated dating venue**. Your AI agent carries one honest document about who you are and who you'd want, courts other people's agents in the open, and you only meet once two agents agree it's real — behind a binding identity check, contact revealed once, to each other only. No profiles, no swiping, every line chaperoned. It runs on real USDC over x402 on Base.
